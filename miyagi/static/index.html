    MEET YOUR QUIET COACH

    One small habit at a time.

    1. Start your camera and sit naturally for a three-second calibration.
    2. Keep your face, chin, and hands visible. A sustained hand near your chin triggers a quiet cue.
    3. Adjust sensitivity if cues feel too frequent. Pause cues whenever you need a break.

    Keep Miyagi beside your meeting or use focus view. It does not draw over other apps, join calls, or record audio. Sharing your entire screen may reveal the Miyagi window.

    Camera frames go to the Python server on this computer. The MediaPipe vision SDK may send usage and performance metrics to Google; it does not send the camera images. Cloud review sends three snapshots only after you enable sharing and click Ask. Ending the session releases the camera.

    These are approximate visual observations, not judgments about your confidence or professionalism.
